# Solution imagery contract

## YF-IMG-02 — Automation Integration

Production source: `automation-integration-final-master.png` (1535 × 1024, approximately 3:2).

- Use as representative solution/service imagery, never as evidence of a specific customer installation, delivered machine, certified safeguarding design or performance result.
- Keep the robot, end-of-arm tooling, central fixture and sensing/conveyor relationship inside every crop.
- The 3:2 master is primary. A 16:9 section crop is approved; near-square crops may omit part of the control cabinet but must preserve the central automation cell.
- Do not treat the HMI or pendant as a readable software screenshot or engineering document.
- Do not bake website copy, logos, metrics, certification marks or labels into the bitmap.
- Keep the master in `src/assets` so Astro emits intrinsic dimensions and responsive AVIF/WebP derivatives.
- Photography direction must remain restrained industrial corporate photography: no cinematic grading, neon, futuristic UI or added technology effects.
